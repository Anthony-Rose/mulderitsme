The Voice Is the Edit

Robert Modell has no makeup, no effect and no dark to hide in. His power is a man talking, filmed as ordinary coverage, which puts the viewer in the same chair as his victims. Pusher is the hour where The X-Files came closest to admitting what a cut does.

The Editor Always Wins

Musings of a Cigarette Smoking Man is a story about a writer whose publisher changed his ending. Its own executives then changed its title, and its planned last shot vanished from the cut. The episode is the series admitting what its conspiracy had always been: an account from a man who needed to have been there.

The Frame Had a Ceiling

The X-Files was composed for the 4:3 television set, and the 2015 remaster that now streams everywhere opened it to 16:9. The wider picture adds a little at the sides and takes the top, and the top was where the show kept its fear.
