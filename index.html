Sheriff Taylor Leaves the Door Unlocked

Glen Morgan and James Wong named a sheriff after Andy Griffith's and then held him to Mayberry habits: no gun, no locks, nothing changes. Home kills him by those rules and gives the Peacocks his nostalgia, down to the song on the car radio.

The Moving Element

Glen Morgan and James Wong wrote a Scully episode after viewers said she needed humanizing, and David Nutter shot it as two faces in a prison room. Beyond the Sea defines Scully's skepticism as a discipline held against her own grief.

The Voice Is the Edit

Robert Modell has no makeup, no effect and no dark to hide in. His power is a man talking, filmed as ordinary coverage, which puts the viewer in the same chair as his victims. Pusher is the hour where The X-Files came closest to admitting what a cut does.
