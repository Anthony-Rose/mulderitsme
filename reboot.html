The Reboot

The reboot, item by item

Hulu has ordered the series. What is confirmed, what the trades have reported and what is still just talk, kept in separate piles.

ConfirmedReportedRumored Updated 2026-10-22 · Full story

  • Series orderConfirmed

    Hulu picked up Ryan Coogler's The X-Files to series on October 13, 2026, eight months after ordering the pilot. Onyx Collective and 20th Television produce with Proximity Media.

  • ShowrunnerConfirmed

    Jennifer Yale (Legion, See, The Copenhagen Test) is showrunner and executive producer, in place since the February pilot order.

  • Coogler's roleConfirmed

    Coogler wrote, directed and executive produced the pilot. On the series he is an executive producer, not the day to day showrunner.

  • LeadsConfirmed

    Danielle Deadwyler and Himesh Patel star as two decorated, mismatched FBI agents assigned to a long shuttered division. Both are co-executive producers.

  • New charactersConfirmed

    Patel said on the record in July that he and Deadwyler play "completely new characters," and Variety's source agrees. Hulu's announcement does not name the roles.

  • ScoreConfirmed

    Ludwig Göransson scored the pilot, per the Proximity founders' statement. Nothing has been said about who scores the rest of the season.

  • CinematographyConfirmed

    Autumn Durald Arkapaw, Coogler's Oscar winning Sinners cinematographer, shot the pilot and posted photos from the Vancouver set. What she shot on has not been stated.

  • Chris CarterConfirmed

    Carter is an executive producer in credit only. He told The Hollywood Reporter in August he had done "exactly nothing" and had chosen not to read the script.

  • Duchovny and AndersonConfirmed

    Anderson said in March she had read the pilot script and liked it. Duchovny said in April there was "nothing concrete" and he did not know whether Mulder exists in the show. No casting announced.

  • PremiereConfirmed

    No premiere date and no episode count. TheWrap's fall 2027 was its own labeled guess; the season still has to be shot.

  • Pilot shootReported

    Vancouver production listings put the pilot shoot at May 11 to June 11, 2026, and Patel confirmed the wrap in early July. No dates for the rest of the season have been announced.

  • Guest castReported

    Deadline reported in May that Amy Madigan, Steve Buscemi, Ben Foster, Devery Jacobs, Lochlyn Munro, Tantoo Cardinal, Joel D. Montgrand and Sofia Grace Clifton have guest roles in the pilot.

  • 65mm and runtimeRumored

    The claim that the pilot runs about two hours and was shot on 65mm film comes from the blog World of Reel, citing unnamed sources. No trade or Hulu confirmation.

  • Mulder and Scully cameoRumored

    World of Reel's October 13 post says Duchovny and Anderson appear in the pilot, attributed only to what the writer has heard. Neither actor nor Hulu has said so.

The truth is out there.
